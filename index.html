FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate are the estimates?

Estimates are structured the way working contractors actually price jobs — itemized labor, materials, and quantities, adjusted to your region. Treat them as a strong, defensible starting point: every line is editable, so you can apply your own rates and job-specific knowledge before anything goes to a client.

What do I need to capture?

A handful of photos covering each wall of the space, plus a short walkthrough video — under a minute is usually plenty. Any modern phone camera works. No laser scanner, measuring hardware, or special app training required.

I'm a homeowner, not a contractor — is RenoBeam for me?

Yes. Homeowners use RenoBeam to get an instant, honest ballpark for a renovation before ever calling a contractor — so you walk into every conversation knowing roughly what the work should cost. Your first 3 estimates are free, no card required.

Does RenoBeam work outside the United States?

RenoBeam is built for a worldwide audience. Regional price adjustment tunes labor and material baselines to your market, and you can override any rate with your own price book. Plans are billed in USD.

Can I edit line items or use my own prices?

Every line item is editable before you send an estimate. On the Crew and Firm plans, price-book customization lets you set your own labor rates and material costs once — and RenoBeam applies them to every estimate after that.

What happens to my photos and videos?

Your captures are used to generate your estimates and stay in your account. They are never sold, and you can delete them at any time. Job-site media is your business data — we treat it that way.
