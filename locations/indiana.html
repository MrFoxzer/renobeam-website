RenoBeam turns a phone walkthrough into a line-item renovation estimate priced for Indiana. Upload photos and a short video of any space and get labor, materials, and quantities back in minutes — adjusted to Indiana market pricing — plus a branded, client-ready PDF proposal.

The local angle

The Indiana market, priced honestly

Ask anyone bidding work in Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, or Evansville: Indiana jobs come with their own conditions. Humid summers and real Midwest winters shape what gets rebuilt and when, and the stock itself — affordable older stock, brick ranches, and steady suburban building around Indianapolis — means no two walkthroughs look alike. RenoBeam was built by a working remodeling contractor who prices exactly this kind of variety for a living, and its estimates are structured the way real bids are.

Because it's cloud software, RenoBeam covers every corner of Indiana from day one. No territory limits, no installs — just capture, upload, and send the proposal.
