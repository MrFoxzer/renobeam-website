Renovation estimating software in Kentucky

In Kentucky, the contractor who quotes first usually wins the job. RenoBeam turns phone photos and a short walkthrough video into a line-item estimate — labor, materials, quantities, Kentucky market pricing — and a branded PDF proposal, in minutes.

Whether you run a remodeling company, a GC operation, a kitchen and bath shop, a handyman service, or a roofing or painting crew serving Kentucky, the goal is the same: quote faster than the competition without guessing at numbers.

The local angle

Why Kentucky contractors quote with RenoBeam

From Louisville to Lexington to Bowling Green, Kentucky contractors deal with shotgun houses, brick ranches, and horse-country properties — and with humidity and storm fronts rolling through the Ohio Valley. That mix punishes lazy estimates. A bid that ignores what Kentucky labor and materials really cost either leaves money on the table or loses the job. RenoBeam adjusts every baseline to your market, then lets you override any line with your own rates.

There's no local office to visit, because there doesn't need to be one: RenoBeam is available everywhere in Kentucky the moment you sign up. If your phone gets a signal on the job site — or even if it doesn't until you're back in coverage — your walkthrough becomes an estimate in minutes.
