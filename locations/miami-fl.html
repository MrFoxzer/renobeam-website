Available in Miami, FL

Renovation estimating software in Miami

RenoBeam is an AI renovation estimator built by a working remodeling contractor — and built to price work the way Miami contractors actually bid it. Photos and a short video in; itemized labor, materials, and Miami market pricing out, with a branded PDF proposal ready to send.

Whether you run a remodeling company, a GC operation, a kitchen and bath shop, a handyman service, or a roofing or painting crew serving Miami, the goal is the same: quote faster than the competition without guessing at numbers.

The local angle

The Miami market, priced honestly

Every Miami contractor knows the stock: condo-board work, impact windows, salt-air corrosion, and recertification-driven renovation demand across older buildings. Pricing that work takes local awareness, and that's what RenoBeam's regional adjustment delivers — labor and material baselines tuned to the Miami market, with your own price book layered on top. Jobs in Coral Gables, Wynwood, Little Havana, and Coconut Grove each get priced like the distinct work they are.

RenoBeam is available throughout Miami, Florida as a cloud product — no office, no hardware, no waiting list by territory. If you can walk the space with a phone, you can bid it the same afternoon.

Features

Built for how Miami bids get won

API access

Estimates don't have to live in another silo: the Firm plan's API feeds them into your existing CRM or claims workflow.

Regional price adjustment

Labor and material baselines tuned to the Miami market, so a bathroom in Coral Gables doesn't get priced like one three states away.

Photo + video capture

No hardware to buy: photos plus a short video from the phone already in your pocket is all the input RenoBeam needs.

Line items, not lump sums

No vague totals. Every scope is itemized with labor, materials, and quantities, structured the way working Miami contractors actually build a bid.

Branded PDF proposals

One tap turns the estimate into a polished proposal with your logo and terms — sent before slower Miami competitors have opened their spreadsheet.

Your price book

Override any rate with your real numbers — your subs, your suppliers, your margins — and RenoBeam applies them to every future Miami estimate.
