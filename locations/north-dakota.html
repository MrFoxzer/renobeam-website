The local angle

Built for the way North Dakota prices work

What makes North Dakota estimating hard isn't the math — it's the variety. The housing stock spans farmsteads, solid small-town homes, and fast-built Fargo-area apartments and suburbs, the weather brings brutal winters and a short, intense building season, and prices in Fargo don't match prices in Minot. RenoBeam handles the variety for you: regional price adjustment tunes labor and material baselines to the market you're actually standing in, and your own price book overrides win every time.

RenoBeam will launch across all of North Dakota as a cloud product — Fargo, Bismarck, Grand Forks, Minot, and West Fargo and everywhere in between — with nothing to install and no hardware beyond the phone already in your pocket.
