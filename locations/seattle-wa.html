The local angle

The Seattle market, priced honestly

The Seattle renovation scene is defined by craftsman remodels, year-round moisture management, backyard DADUs, and some of the highest labor rates in the country. That's exactly the kind of market where itemized, locally priced estimates win: clients here see plenty of bids, and the vague lump-sum quote is the first one in the trash. RenoBeam breaks every job into the line items Seattle contractors actually price — from a kitchen in Ballard to a bath in West Seattle to a full-gut in Queen Anne.

It's a cloud product that will launch across Seattle, Washington and its suburbs with nothing to install. Walk the space, upload the capture, send the proposal — often before your competitor has booked their site visit.
