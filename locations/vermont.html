The local angle

Estimating in Vermont is its own animal

What makes Vermont estimating hard isn't the math — it's the variety. The housing stock spans farmhouses, capes, and village homes that predate modern framing, the weather brings long winters and old bones, and prices in Burlington don't match prices in Stowe. RenoBeam handles the variety for you: regional price adjustment tunes labor and material baselines to the market you're actually standing in, and your own price book overrides win every time.

RenoBeam is available across all of Vermont as a cloud product — Burlington, Montpelier, Rutland, and Stowe and everywhere in between — with nothing to install and no hardware beyond the phone already in your pocket.
